**Reading the ablation (differences below ~0.6 pt are within one fold-std).** *Emojis:* removing emojis costs 0.3 pt for the n-gram model; every meaning-aware representation (meaning token, Urdu gloss, meaning+category+polarity, symbol+meaning) is within noise of the raw symbol. The reason is corpus-specific: once words such as a politician's name, طنز / مزاح, or ironic praise are present the emoji is redundant for a bag-of-words model; the emoji decides the short, lexically neutral posts (see error analysis), and the neural models, which see emoji *sequences*, gain ~1 pt from keeping them (TextCNN 0.797 vs 0.788). The meaning representations matter for a different reason — rare emojis: 538 distinct emojis occur, 60 % of them fewer than five times, and mapping them to a category / polarity / Urdu meaning lets the model generalise from 😅 to 😓, which the raw symbol cannot. *Features:* char n-grams beat word n-grams (spelling variation, suffix morphology, no reliable spacing) and the two are complementary (+0.6 pt); the 72 hand-crafted features alone reach 0.756 and add a small consistent gain on top. *Normalisation:* Urdu character normalisation is the best single setting (+0.15 pt over raw, lowest variance); the social-media steps (laughter / punctuation spacing) are neutral, so rhetorical punctuation is kept. *Artefacts:* 842 of 12 382 train+val posts still contain the hashtags used to harvest the sarcastic class (#طنز, #مزاح, #sarcasm) and 835 of them are labelled sarcastic; removing these tokens lowers Macro-F1 by 0.6 pt, i.e. all reported scores are slightly optimistic relative to in-the-wild performance and the honest number is ≈ 0.815. *Transformer emoji input:* raw emoji 0.811, Urdu meaning only 0.809, symbol + meaning 0.806 Macro-F1 on test — also within noise: XLM-R's SentencePiece vocabulary already carries usable representations for the frequent emojis, and the Urdu gloss mainly helps the ~40 % of emoji types that are rare. The practical value of the meaning layer is therefore less in the headline score than in (a) robustness to rare emojis, (b) the dense contrast features for the classical/neural models, and (c) explainability: every prediction can be returned with the meaning of its emojis and the cues that fired.
